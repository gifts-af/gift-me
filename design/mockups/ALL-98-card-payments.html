5b · Consent wording (question 2)

Plain text, about 400 characters (Stripe allows 1,200). Covers when we charge and how the amount is set.

Proposed

GiftsAF keeps this card to buy gifts you approve. We charge it only when you tap Buy now or Buy again, or on an auto-buy date we emailed you about. Each charge is for one gift: its price, shipping and tax, plus the GiftsAF fee. It's never more than the “Most you’ll pay” we showed you. Saving a card doesn’t buy anything. You can remove it anytime in Settings. Charges show as GIFTSAF.

Why each part: when we charge (Buy now, Buy again, or an auto-buy date we emailed about); how much (one gift plus the fee, capped by the number we showed); saving buys nothing (owner's 2026-11-25 rule); how to stop (Remove in Settings); and the statement name, so the charge isn't a surprise.

5c · Statement name (question 3)

Proposed: GIFTSAF. 7 characters, within Stripe's 5 to 22, matches the brand and the domain. Set once in our Stripe account. [Verify] that Zinc Connect charges carry our name, not Zinc's, on the first real card order.
