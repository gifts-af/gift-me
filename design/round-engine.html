GiftsAF Round Engine
Gifts

How a gift round moves

Every gift starts as a round: one person, one occasion, one year. The round engine walks it from "scheduled" to "complete" with a once-a-day tick, and user actions and Zinc webhooks move it forward in between. The tick hands each due round to its own function call through a queue, so a busy morning fits (ALL-125). Gift ideas come from the idea engine (ALL-27), after anything that buys. For someone who keeps a gift list, the round offers gifts from that list first and only asks the AI when fewer than 3 qualify (ALL-29).

1 · Lifecycle

Nine statuses: a main path and three side statuses

moves forward rejected, ideas regenerate something needs the buyer deadline passes

Every move writes gift_rounds.status and next_action_at together, so the app and recovery only ever read the database. Held orders (funds, setup, paused) wait in needs_attention and are bought once covered. At the deadline, held gifts are cancelled; the round moves to ordered if any gift is already placed, otherwise skipped. An order Zinc hasn't confirmed yet keeps the round in needs_attention. A held gift decides the round's reason before a failed one, and every failed gift is told by its own key whatever the reason (ALL-116). A round that can't get ideas never moves to needs_attention: it stays in generating and skips at its deadline (ALL-27). Rounds are only made for buyers with paid features while ideas cost money (decision 53). skipped never moves again; the next occasion starts a fresh round. A changed date (T92): changing a birthday or anniversary ends its open round as skipped when it has no orders, or only failed ones, and frees its place for the year, so the next due tick can start one for the real date. A round with a gift keeps its date. None of these (ALL-29): when a new search can still run, the ideas are rejected, the round's sent steps are renamed (heads_up@1, reminder_1@1…) and it goes back to generating, so the new ideas get their own heads-up and reminders. When no search is left (runs used up, too close to ship-by, engine off, or nothing to search with), the reason is saved and the current ideas stay buyable. Earlier ideas come back (ALL-167): if the new search then finds nothing and no other run can start, the latest rejected set is put back on offer, less gifts that can't arrive in time or were ever ordered, claimed or unshared; the round gets a heads-up and moves to awaiting_approval. A search that found nothing isn't repeated until the buyer gives a new fact. A failed gift is fixed on its own page, /rounds/<id>/fix/<order>, through replace_failed_order (T81). Ways out of a failed gift (ALL-185): when the failed gift is the round's only one still in play, "Pick a different gift" resolves it and moves the round back to awaiting_approval (drop_failed_order), due again at once so its reminders carry on; and while nothing is held, being ordered or on its way, "Skip" closes the round as skipped through the hold emails' own close (skip_failed_round, then cancel_held), under the lease. Neither sends a later email about a failure the page showed, except a store cancel's refund, which the daily news still tells. Tracking (ALL-33, T89): a gift that may arrive late stays ordered; the buyer is told in the daily summary with their options, never moved to needs_attention. A store cancel before ship-by goes through the fix page; one after ship-by is told plainly with Zinc's refund amount, and nothing more is bought. The round moves to delivered once every gift has arrived, by Zinc's event or the buyer's "Mark as arrived". Reactions (ALL-34, T91): the day after the occasion, or the day after the round became delivered if that was later, the buyer is asked how each delivered gift went. The round moves to complete when every delivered gift has an answer, or 7 days after it was due to be asked. A round still ordered is never asked or closed (ALL-33). Source: technical-design §5 and §6.6, reconcile_round, save_reaction, close_reacted_rounds, T85, T89, T91.

2 · The daily tick

What runs every morning

Vercel Cron, 12:00 UTC

Calls GET /api/rounds/tick with Bearer $CRON_SECRET. 8 a.m. Eastern in summer, 7 a.m. in winter. Production only. After the secret check it writes today's row in tick_heartbeats (started, then finished with its result and failure counts), so the health check can see a missed or failed tick (T87).

0Housekeeping

Order checks, retrying "ordered" and "delivered" emails, clearing old notes and tracking numbers, the daily tracking summary (one email per buyer with late, back-on-time and late store-cancel news, ALL-33), then reactions: rounds that are answered or out of time are closed, and each buyer gets one email asking how their delivered gifts went (ALL-34). Always runs, whatever the round switches say; tracking and reactions have their own switches.

Yes

1Create due rounds

One per occasion whose start date has arrived, for buyers with paid features. A unique key on person, occasion and year stops duplicates. A failure logs round_create_failed and the tick carries on.

2Send due rounds to the queue

Reads rounds with next_action_at <= now() and no lock, without claiming them. Sends one Vercel Queues message per round, and one per buyer for all of that buyer's held gifts, so one wallet is spent in deadline order. Each message is signed with the cron secret, and keyed per round (or buyer) per day, so a re-run tick doesn't send it twice. Logs round_fanout; failed sends alert as round_fanout_send_failed and those rounds wait for tomorrow.

Each message: its own function call, up to 5 at once

3Claim and advance

Checks the signature, then claims the round with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED and a 10-minute lock, so a webhook, a buyer or a second delivery can't work on it at the same time. If someone else holds it, the call just stops. No time limit here: anything that buys runs exactly as before. The step does whatever is due:

Buy held gifts, send remindersonly the latest overdue reminder if ticks were missed
Ask for an addressor go on to step 4 for ideas
Skip at the deadlineemailed if the buyer was asked, or a paid round ran a search; not after the too-late email
Then: write the new status, set the next next_action_at, clear the lock. Logs round_job: waiting when no step finished, with what the idea run came to. Only a buyer's message can place gifts (T93).

4Idea run

Only if idea_engine_enabled = 1, under the same claim. One run per call, with a deadline of tick_deadline_ms (270 s) from the call's start; AI and Zinc calls stop there. At most idea_round_runs_per_day round runs start each day across all calls; past that, the round is released for tomorrow and ideas.daily_limit_reached alerts. Each run is counted before it spends and capped at idea_run_max_cents. Zinc's request rate is shared across the calls running at once.

Rejection notices

Sent by the tick after the rounds are queued.

Off switch

With round_fanout_enabled = 0, nothing is queued: the tick claims and runs steps 3 and 4 itself, one round after another, as before ALL-125. Set it to 0 if the queue misbehaves. idea_busy_retry_seconds = 0 stops only the same-day follow-ups.

Health check, 14:00 UTC

/api/health reads the heartbeat and the database two hours later and emails the owner only when something needs them; Mondays always send a summary (T87, ALL-35). Its first query keeps the free database awake.

Same step, no waiting

These call the step function right away:

  • Buy (approve) or fix a failed gift
  • Zinc webhook
  • Auto top-up (retries holds)

A saved address, a new fact, or None of these waits for the next tick. The tick is the safety net.

Recorded before sent

Each email is written to round_sends first. A crash in between means it's never sent: a missed reminder beats a duplicate.

Refused, then retried

If the email service cleanly refuses, the record is removed and the next tick tries again.

Still ours before each send

The engine checks its lease just before sending. If a date change ended the round, it stops quietly and logs round_ended_by_move (T92).

The skip never waits

A refused skip email doesn't hold the round. It still ends skipped, logs round_skip_email_failed, and isn't retried.

Every failure told once

Each failed gift has its own key, fail:<order id>. Several at once go in one summary. Any close hands untold ones to the "we couldn't buy it" email (ALL-116), except a store cancel, which is always told with the store wording.

Tracking news told once

Each gift's late, back-on-time and late-cancel news has its own key (late:, ontime:, cancel_late:), sent from the daily run only. A refused summary gives every key back (ALL-33).

Ideas never delay a purchase

The deadline bounds only the idea run. Held gifts are bought with no time limit, as before ideas existed (T68, T75).

A round runs once at a time

The queue may deliver a message twice. The claim makes the second one stop, and placing checks the claim again, so nothing is bought twice (T76).

Source: technical-design §5 and §5.1 (T8, T49, T68, T75, T76, T87, ALL-28 rulings 12–13, ALL-116, ALL-27, ALL-125, ALL-35, T93).

3 · The clock

When each step is due

Days are counted back from the ship-by deadline. Each step is just a date in next_action_at; nothing sleeps or waits. A late start compresses this and drops reminders that no longer fit.

Timings live in app_settings, not code. On the last tick before the ship-by day, a search that finds nothing is the round's last: the buyer gets one "too late" email, and the close at the deadline then sends no skipped email (ALL-167). Source: technical-design §4.1, §5.1.
